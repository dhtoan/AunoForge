# Code of Conduct

AunoForge is built for collaborative open-source maintenance. Contributors and maintainers are expected to communicate respectfully, review technical claims on evidence, and make room for people with different experience levels and backgrounds.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, deliberate disclosure of another person's secrets or private repository data, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove abusive content; close disruptive threads; reject contributions; or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate, documented where appropriate, and avoid publishing sensitive reporter information.

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers rather than escalating sensitive details in a public issue.
